Asia Follower — Child Safety Standards

Effective: 31 October 2026. Applies to the Asia Follower Android application, package com.asiafollower.app.

Prohibited conduct

Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, trafficking and other conduct that sexually exploits or endangers children are prohibited. The application is intended for adults aged 18 and over; this does not remove these protections.

Report a concern

In the app, open “Gizlilik ve destek” then “Çocuk güvenliği bildirimi”. The responsible operator monitors tokafatma997@gmail.com. Describe the concern and, when relevant, provide the page URL. Do not send abuse images, Instagram passwords or unnecessary sensitive information about a child. The app does not automatically attach your username or screen to an email. In an immediate emergency, contact local emergency services.

Response and reporting

The responsible operator reviews reports, takes appropriate action on confirmed child-safety violations, and removes or disables access to offending content within the application, or works with the party able to do so. Where applicable, the operator reports confirmed CSAM to the relevant regional or national authorities in accordance with applicable law. Reports about externally hosted content may require coordination with that provider; this does not remove responsibility for access through the app.
